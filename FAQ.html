Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I start investing?

Register an account, verify your identity, fund your wallet with Bitcoin or other supported cryptocurrencies, and choose an investment plan.

What’s the minimum investment amount?

You can start with as little as $20 worth of Bitcoin.

Do I need experience to invest?

No. BitInvest is built for beginners and professionals alike.

Investment & Returns

How long before I get ROI?

Our plans run from 7–30 days depending on your selected tier. Returns are credited automatically once the plan matures.

Can I withdraw before maturity?

Funds must stay locked until plan completion for optimal returns. Contact support for exceptions.

Is my profit fixed or variable?

Profits are fixed based on your plan's ROI percentage.

Security & Accounts

Is my money safe?

Yes, we use advanced encryption, cold wallet storage, and 2FA for maximum security.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the password reset option. You’ll receive a secure link by email.

Deposits & Withdrawals

Which payment methods do you accept?

BTC, USDT, ETH, and bank transfers.

Typically 30 minutes to 24 hours depending on network confirmation.
